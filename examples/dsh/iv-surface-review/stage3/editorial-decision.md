# Stage 3 — Editorial Decision

**Manuscript:** *Arbitrage-Free Implied Volatility Surface Construction: A Structured Literature Review of Parametric, Nonparametric, and Neural Approaches* (v1.2)

**Decision:** **Major Revision**

## Synthesis

The five reviews converge on a common diagnosis: the paper's guarantee taxonomy and static/dynamic separation are worth publishing, but the paper is not yet positioned, auditable, or falsifiable enough for a review venue. The reviews do not ask for a new search or new data; they ask for documentation, mapping, and framing. That is a recoverable revision.

## Major issues (must address)

1. **J1 / P1 — audience and review type.** State the primary reader and the review type (structured narrative, not systematic, not PRISMA, not registered).
2. **M1 — reproducibility.** Add a 24-query log and a 40-source inclusion/evidence mapping; label the 351 → 40 flow as a rationale, not a PRISMA audit.
3. **M2 / D1 — evidence and taxonomy mapping.** Add Appendix D (source, method family, enforcement family, static/dynamic test, evidence level) and mark reported claims as reported, not tested.
4. **D2 — dynamic evidence.** Add a dynamic-evidence table and tie the “frontier” claim to it.
5. **DA1 / DA2 — so-what and falsifiability.** Add “what changes for the reader,” a testable-proposition table, and a “what would change this map” paragraph.
6. **J2 / P2 / P3 — proposal status.** Keep “proposed, not validated” on the seven-dimension matrix and the decision guide; add a use case and a validation agenda.

## Minor issues

- Add JEL codes if the venue expects them.
- Shorten the running head; keep the title.
- Standardize “taxonomy / matrix / framework.”
- Explain the 2026/2027 online-first years.
- Disclose the zh-CN abstract deviation.
- Reduce repeated “no ranking” statements.

## What the revision must not do

- Do not add a new search or a new corpus; the 40-source freeze is part of the scope.
- Do not claim PRISMA compliance or a systematic review.
- Do not convert the taxonomy into a validated benchmark.
- Do not add a method ranking.

## Next step

Stage 4 revision with a point-by-point response to this decision and the five reviews. The revision roadmap is in `revision-roadmap.md`. A Stage 3' re-review will verify the major issues; the paper then goes to Stage 4.5 integrity and Stage 5 finalization.
