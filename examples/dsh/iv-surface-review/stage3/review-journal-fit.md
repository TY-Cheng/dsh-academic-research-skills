# Stage 3 — Journal-Fit Review

**Recommendation:** Major Revision

## Summary

The paper synthesizes 40 sources on arbitrage-free implied-volatility-surface construction and proposes a four-family enforcement taxonomy plus a seven-dimension evaluation matrix. The topic is timely, the writing is clear, and the static/dynamic separation is useful. The paper is not yet positioned for a specific venue, and its most practical element—the decision guide—needs a clearer status.

## Strengths

- The guarantee taxonomy is intuitive and cuts across method families.
- The paper is candid about its evidence limits (40/351, 10 preprints, abstract-level).
- The decision guide and research agenda give the reader something to use.
- The tables are well structured; the six-table set is coherent.

## Major issues

**J1. Audience and venue are underspecified.** “General academic journal / arXiv-style” is not a positioning. A finance review journal, a quant-finance survey venue, and arXiv imply different audiences. State the primary reader (model risk/model validation, benchmark design, or academic research) and adjust the introduction and abstract accordingly.

**J2. The decision guide may be read as validated advice.** Table 5 is introduced as a “proposed aid,” but the cells read as recommendations (“should prefer,” “should add”). Keep the proposal language in the table caption, the surrounding text, and the conclusion; add a sentence that the guide has not been validated by a user study or a benchmark.

**J3. The contribution is modest but the abstract still sounds like a framework paper.** The abstract says the review “proposes” a taxonomy and a matrix; that is accurate, but the reader may expect a validated framework. Foreground “corpus-scoped map and research agenda” in the abstract and the first paragraph.

## Minor issues

- The title is long; a shorter running head would help.
- Add JEL codes (e.g., G13, C45, C63) if the venue expects them.
- The keywords are good; consider adding “model risk” and “benchmark design.”
- The paper says “no method ranking” several times; keep one strong statement and remove the repeats.

## What would make it acceptable

A clear audience/venue statement, a “proposed, not validated” status for the decision guide, and a contribution statement that matches the review genre. The Stage 4 revision can do this without new data.
