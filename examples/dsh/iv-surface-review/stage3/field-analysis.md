# Stage 3 — Field Analysis

**Manuscript:** *Arbitrage-Free Implied Volatility Surface Construction: A Structured Literature Review of Parametric, Nonparametric, and Neural Approaches* (v1.2)

**Target:** general academic journal / arXiv-style preprint; no specific venue.

## Field expectations

Quantitative-finance venues reward identification, data, and a benchmark. A review is judged by whether it changes how the field sees the literature: a new taxonomy, a new testable distinction, or a synthesis that resolves apparent contradictions. The paper's strongest asset is the guarantee-based taxonomy and the static/dynamic separation. Its weakest asset is evidentiary: 40 sources, 10 preprints, abstract-level, single database, no full text.

## Fit

Moderate. The paper is not a top-three empirical finance paper, and it does not claim to be. It fits a review-oriented finance journal, a quant-finance venue with a survey section, or arXiv. The paper should name its audience (model risk, model validation, benchmark design) and state the "so what" in the first two pages. The decision guide is the most practitioner-relevant element; it needs an explicit "proposed, not validated" status.

## Novelty

The cross-family guarantee taxonomy is a useful organizing device. The static/dynamic separation is not new in itself, but the claim that static admissibility is necessary yet insufficient, and that dynamic testing is the missing layer, is a sharp synthesis. The seven-dimension matrix is a proposal; its value depends on adoption, not on the paper alone.

## Main risk

A reviewer may say the paper is a well-organized survey with a taxonomy that is descriptive rather than explanatory. The Stage 4 revision should add a falsifiable proposition table and a "what would change this map" paragraph, and should map all 40 sources to the taxonomy so the classification is auditable.
