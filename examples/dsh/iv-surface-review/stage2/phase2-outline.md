A structured literature review for a general finance / quantitative-finance readership. The reader knows implied-volatility modelling but not the full construction literature. The paper's job is to make the guarantee structure legible, not to sell a method.

## Argument order

1. “Arbitrage-free” is not one property; it is a family of guarantees.
2. The organizing variable is where the constraint enters the pipeline, not the method family.
3. Parametric static guarantees are explicit but conditional; neural/generative guarantees are method-specific and rarely tested dynamically.
4. Dynamic no-arbitrage is a separate, under-tested layer.
5. Evaluation is fragmented; a seven-dimension matrix is proposed, not validated.
6. The contribution is a map and a research agenda, not a ranking.

## Constraints

- English body; Chinese abstract; APA 7.0.
- No new search; 40 frozen sources.
- No method ranking; no PRISMA claim; no causal claims about superiority.
- Every claim cited; 0 citation orphans; 40 references; 40-row Appendix B.
- Word count within 6,000–8,000 body (excluding abstract, declarations, references, appendices).
