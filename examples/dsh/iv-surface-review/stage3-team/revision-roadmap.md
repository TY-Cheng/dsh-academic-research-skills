## Re-review protocol

1. Check M1–M6 against the revised text and appendices; a response letter alone is not sufficient.
2. Check each minor item in the location listed.
3. Re-run the claim-ladder test on the abstract, findings, conclusion, and §5.3.
4. Confirm the corpus and evidence disclosures were not weakened.
5. Confirm no new literature search was added without a corresponding corpus update and provenance note.

## Decision note

The editorial decision is Major Revision, not Reject, because the core reframing survives and no new data are required. It is not Accept or Minor Revision because M1–M6 are load-bearing for the paper's contribution claims. If M2 or M4 cannot be completed within the declared scope, the paper should be repositioned as a commentary or agenda piece and the empirical/coding claims dropped rather than asserted.
