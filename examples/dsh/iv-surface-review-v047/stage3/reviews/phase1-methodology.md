## Contract Paraphrase

D1 methodology_rigor asks whether the design, data handling, reporting, and reproducibility affordances of this study would clear a methods-focused referee bar. Read as a producer of evidence, the manuscript must show how its evidence base was assembled, how records were screened and counted, how material was extracted and coded, and whether an independent researcher could rerun those steps and arrive at the same corpus and the same findings. Because a review study has no experimental design to inspect, the protocol itself supplies the design: pre-specified selection rules, auditable screening and exclusion accounting, explicit source appraisal, and a documented analysis path from sources to synthesis. The bar is reproducibility of the reasoning chain, not novelty of data.

D2 domain_accuracy asks whether the manuscript's substantive claims match the current state of knowledge in its field, whether prior work is characterised faithfully rather than through convenient summary, and whether domain terminology, models, and reported results are used without factual error. From the methodology seat I read this as an evidence-fidelity question: every attribution to a prior source should be traceable to what that source actually establishes, and no technical statement should be stronger than the source or the derivation that carries it.

D3 argumentative_coherence asks whether the central thesis holds together on its own terms: whether premises are consistent with one another, whether the evidence assembled actually bears on the claims drawn from it, whether any fallacy does damage to the central line of reasoning, and whether a premise the thesis depends on is left unsupported. The clause about unsupported premises reaches claims about who benefits, who bears costs, or who is excluded; such distributional premises need evidence like any other, not assertion. I read this dimension as testing the load-bearing steps of the argument, not the elegance of its prose.

D4 cross_disciplinary_relevance asks whether framing, definitions, and implications are intelligible to readers from adjacent fields and whether the manuscript's interdisciplinary claims are substantiated rather than gestured at. The test is whether a competent outsider can follow the problem, the terms, and the stakes without already sharing the author's specialism, and whether any borrowing across fields is argued rather than assumed.

D5 writing_and_structure asks whether the manuscript is organised so that a reader can follow the argument, whether exposition is clear, whether figures and tables carry their evidentiary weight, and whether the manuscript follows the conventions of its target venue. For a methods assessment this is instrumental: unclear structure or unreadable exhibits can hide an unreproducible chain, so I check whether the organisation makes the protocol and the evidence inspectable.

D6 venue_fit_and_contribution asks whether the manuscript belongs in the configured venue and whether it offers an original, significant contribution for that readership. Absent an author-confirmed venue target, I make no venue-alignment claim; the contribution question is treated as whether the manuscript adds something beyond restating its sources, judged against the scope the contract itself describes.

## Scoring Plan

### D1: methodology_rigor

dimension_id: D1
what_to_look_for: Whether the evidence base is assembled by a documented and reproducible protocol: search strategy, inclusion and exclusion rules, screening counts that reconcile, extraction and coding procedures, source appraisal, and enough procedural detail for an independent analyst to rerun the review and reach the same corpus and conclusions.
what_triggers_block: The protocol is not reconstructable or the reported screening accounting does not reconcile, so the included corpus cannot be tied to stated selection rules and the study's evidence base is unauditable as presented.
what_triggers_warn: Reproducibility is only partial: one analyst performs screening or coding without a reliability check, extraction rests on abstracts where fuller evidence exists, or per-source appraisal is thin enough that a reader cannot see why each source was admitted.
what_triggers_fatal: The evidence base cannot support the central claim at all: sources are misrepresented, the corpus is assembled by an undisclosed selection process, or the review's own protocol would exclude material on which its headline conclusion depends.

### D3: argumentative_coherence

dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Whether the central thesis is internally consistent, whether each core claim is supported by evidence the manuscript actually presents, whether inference steps are valid, and whether premises the argument depends on, including distributional claims about beneficiaries, costs, or exclusions, are themselves evidenced.
what_triggers_block: A load-bearing claim depends on an inference the manuscript's own evidence contradicts or on a premise left entirely unsupported, so removing or correcting that step collapses the central argument as stated.
what_triggers_warn: The conclusion outruns the assembled evidence: cross-source synthesis asserts a general or uniform pattern where the cited material supports only conditional, heterogeneous, or indirect findings, or the manuscript's scope shifts between evidence types without acknowledgement.
what_triggers_fatal: The central thesis is self-contradictory or rests on a premise the manuscript itself refutes, leaving no coherent argument for the stated conclusion even after charitable reconstruction.
