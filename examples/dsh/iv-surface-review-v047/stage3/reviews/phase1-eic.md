## Contract Paraphrase

D1, methodology rigor: from the editor's chair I will ask whether the study design that produced the manuscript's claims is described precisely enough that an independent reader could reconstruct the evidentiary path, whether data handling and reported statistics are internally auditable, and whether the authors disclose the material limits of what their design can establish. I will not re-run their analyses, but I will check that the route from raw material to reported result is stated rather than assumed.

D2, domain accuracy: I will check whether the manuscript's substantive claims are consistent with the state of the field it addresses, whether prior work is characterised in a way its own cited sources would recognise, and whether specialist terminology and reported results are free of factual slips. A paper can be beautifully organised and still misstate the literature, so this dimension asks whether an informed reader in the field would trust the content.

D3, argumentative coherence: I will trace whether the central thesis holds together from premise to conclusion, whether the presented evidence actually bears the weight the argument places on it, and whether any load-bearing premise, including claims about who gains, who pays, or who is excluded, is left unsupported. My concern is the internal logic of the case as constructed, not the popularity of its conclusion.

D4, cross-disciplinary relevance: I will ask whether an intelligent reader from an adjacent field could follow the framing, definitions, and implications without specialised induction, and whether any claim that reaches across disciplinary boundaries is substantiated rather than merely asserted. Accessible framing is an editorial value here, not a demand to dilute the technical content.

D5, writing and structure: I will assess whether the manuscript can be read as a coherent document, whether its sections advance a legible argument, whether figures and tables are self-explanatory and correctly tied to the claims they support, and whether the prose and formatting respect the conventions of the target field. Presentation defects matter here only to the extent that they impede editorial comprehension or signal carelessness about the reader.

D6, venue fit and contribution: I will judge whether the manuscript states a contribution that is genuinely original and significant for the readership it addresses, and whether the advance it claims is delivered by its own contents rather than promised in the abstract. Because no venue target has been confirmed for this review, I will not manufacture a fit verdict against an imagined journal; the commitment below records that limitation.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure

dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: Whether the manuscript reads as one coherent argument from title through conclusion, whether sections and exhibits are ordered and cross-referenced so an editor can follow the case without reconstructing it, and whether prose, captions, and formatting meet the presentation conventions of the target field.
what_triggers_block: Structural or expository failures that stop an editor from following the argument at all, such as missing or contradictory sections, exhibits that cannot be matched to the claims they are said to support, or prose that obscures the central thesis throughout the manuscript.
what_triggers_warn: Recurring but localised defects in organisation, exposition, or exhibit presentation that slow comprehension without preventing reconstruction of the argument, such as undefined abbreviations, thin figure captions, inconsistent terminology, or abrupt transitions between sections.

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution

dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: Whether the manuscript articulates an original contribution of field-level significance and then delivers it, with a clear advance over the work it engages and implications that matter to the readership it addresses.
what_triggers_block: The claimed contribution is absent, contradicted by the manuscript's own contents, or so substantially duplicative of work it cites that no defensible field-level advance remains.
what_triggers_warn: The contribution exists but is incremental, narrowly framed, or asserted more strongly than the presented evidence establishes, so significance for the field is overstated rather than absent.
what_triggers_fatal: The manuscript's central contribution is unsupportable because its own analysis contradicts the stated result or because the work it presents is a restatement of a source it treats as prior literature.
