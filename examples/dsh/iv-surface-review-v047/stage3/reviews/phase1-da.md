## Contract Paraphrase

D1 methodology_rigor. From the adversarial seat, this dimension asks whether the study design, data handling, statistical reporting, and reproducibility affordances would survive an attempt to re-execute the review from what the paper itself discloses. The challenge is not whether a method is fashionable but whether the reported procedure is specific enough that an independent party could repeat it, whether the selection and coding steps are auditable rather than merely narrated, and whether any inferential statistic or quantitative summary is reported with the conditions that would let a sceptic check it. A paper can satisfy this dimension by disclosing its procedure fully even where the design is interpretive; it fails when the reader must trust an undisclosed step, when a reported quantity has no traceable basis, or when the affordances for checking the work are asserted rather than supplied.

D2 domain_accuracy. From the adversarial seat, this dimension asks whether the paper's substantive claims agree with the current state of the field, whether prior work is represented as its authors would recognise it, and whether domain-specific terms, results, and mechanisms are used without error. The challenge is to look for a claim that is superficially plausible but wrong at the level of the underlying theory, a citation whose finding is reversed or stripped of its conditions, a construct that quietly changes meaning between sections, or a result attributed to a source that does not contain it. Correct representation is not the same as favourable representation: a source may be cited accurately yet placed in a role its design cannot support, and that mismatch is a domain-accuracy defect even when the quotation is exact.

D3 argumentative_coherence. From the adversarial seat, this dimension asks whether the central thesis holds together internally, whether the evidence actually presented licenses the claims built on it, and whether any fallacy breaks the chain from premise to conclusion. My specific charge is to test the load-bearing steps: whether a claim about absence rests on an adequate search rather than on not having looked, whether a claim about a mechanism is inferred from a description of practice, whether a normative conclusion is smuggled in behind a descriptive finding, and whether the argument depends on a premise the paper never states, including a premise about who benefits, who bears the enforcement cost, or who is left out. A coherence pass is not a compliment about clear writing; it means the inference survives the strongest reading against it.

D4 cross_disciplinary_relevance. From the adversarial seat, this dimension asks whether the framing, definitions, and implications are accessible to a reader from an adjacent field and whether any interdisciplinary claim is substantiated rather than gestured at. The challenge is to check that borrowed constructs are defined where they are used, that a term with different meanings in neighbouring literatures is not allowed to equivocate, and that a claim of relevance to another discipline is backed by something more than an assertion that the topic matters. Accessibility is not simplification: a paper may be demanding and still pass, provided the reader is given the definitions and the bridge premises needed to follow the argument.

D5 writing_and_structure. From the adversarial seat, this dimension asks whether the organisation, exposition, figures, tables, and conformity to venue conventions let a reader locate and check the argument without reconstructing it. The challenge is to find structure that hides a gap: a section that promises synthesis and delivers summary, a table whose caption overstates what its cells show, a figure that is decorative rather than evidential, or a formatting choice that makes a claim look supported when the support sits elsewhere. Presentation matters here only where it changes what a reader can verify; cosmetic preferences are not defects, but an organisational choice that obscures an inferential step is.

D6 venue_fit_and_contribution. From the adversarial seat, this dimension asks whether the work would fit a configured venue and whether it makes an original, significant contribution for that readership. Because no target venue is bound in this run, I can test only the contribution side: what the paper adds beyond the sources it synthesises, whether the addition is substantive rather than a relabelling of existing categories, and whether the claimed scope matches the evidence offered. I make no venue-alignment or submission-readiness claim, and I treat the absence of a binding as a limit on this dimension rather than as a finding against the paper.

## Scoring Plan

### D3: argumentative_coherence
dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: whether each step from the stated evidence to the central inference is licensed, whether rival explanations and contrary evidence are actually excluded, and whether the argument leans on an unstated premise about who benefits, who pays, or who is left out.
what_triggers_block: the central inference does not follow from the evidence presented, or a load-bearing premise about benefit, cost, or exclusion is left unsupported and cannot be repaired without new evidence or a changed claim.
what_triggers_warn: the central inference survives only with an unstated qualification, a rival explanation is not excluded, or a load-bearing premise is asserted without support but is repairable by argument alone.
what_triggers_fatal: the paper's core claim is contradicted by its own reported evidence, or the argument's decisive premise is false as stated, so no revision short of abandoning the claim can restore it.
