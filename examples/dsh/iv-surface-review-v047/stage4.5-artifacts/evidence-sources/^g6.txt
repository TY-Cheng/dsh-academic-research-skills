Static no-arbitrage conditions on implied volatility surfaces are usually imposed through a parametric form or a training penalty. Parametric fits to sparse, noisy quotes are not auto- matically arbitrage-free, and penalties give no confidence away from the points where they are applied. As a proof of concept, we ask whether convexity in log-moneyness and monotonicity in Maturity can be built into a neural total-variance surface by architecture while it stays trainable. with automatic differentiation, and what this costs in accuracy. The network is positive, con- vex in log-moneyness and non-decreasing in maturity at every input; it combines input-convex blocks gated by non-negative maturity ramps and uses an output-scale calibration at initializa- tion. We test it on synthetic data from rejection-sampled arbitrage-free SVI slices with Gaussian noise (standard deviation 0.02) and stratified masking, in three regimes with five seeds each, against per-slice SVI, SSVI, a soft-penalty MLP, and a Dugas-style ablation, on clean data and under a calendar-inversion stress test. On clean data the hard-constrained model has 0.00%. convexity, Durrleman, and calendar violations in all 15 runs (tolerance 10⁻⁴). The soft penalty MLP shows 0.00–0.04% convexity, 0.00–0.84% Durrleman, and 0.00–0.02% calendar violations and per-slice SVI shows 2.69–3.88% Durrleman and 17.86–31.02% calendar violations. The guarantee costs accuracy: overall RMSE in total variance is 0.0099, 0.0124, and 0.0176 (normal, high-skew, stressed) against 0.0074, 0.0096, and 0.0135 for SSVI and 0.0092, 0.0112, and 0.0154 for the soft-penalty MLP. Removing the output-scale calibration raises RMSE by about an or der of magnitude. Under a calendar-inversion stress test, the hard-constrained model again has 0.00% violations, against 0.20% calendar violations for the soft-penalty MLP and 29.86% for per-slice SVI, and every model’s accuracy degrades. The data are synthetic and well specified for SVI; convexity and monotonicity are guaranteed, whereas Durrleman’s condition is audited empirically.
