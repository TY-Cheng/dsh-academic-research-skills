We investigate the absence of static arbitrage in models describing the stochastic evolution of implied volatility surfaces. For Gaussian factor models, we derive explicit formulas for the probabilities of calendar spread, call spread and butterfly arbitrage, showing that these probabilities are strictly positive under suitable non-degeneracy conditions. We then develop a framework based on bounded stochastic factors and establish deterministic necessary and sufficient conditions for the absence of static arbitrage, together with simpler sufficient conditions. As an application, we construct an arbitrage free modification of the Cont and Vuletić model using Jacobi diffusions, preserving its main empirical features while eliminating static arbitrage.
