Implied volatility surfaces play a fundamental role in the pricing and risk management of financial derivatives. However, as a result of estimation noise, they often contain arbitrage violations which need to be corrected to allow for effective pricing. Traditional smoothing techniques struggle to scale efficiently and usually need to be manually re-fitted to enforce no-arbitrage conditions. In this paper, we propose a novel deep learning framework that utilizes Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) to smooth implied volatility surfaces while enforcing arbitrage constraints. Our approach transforms noisy, arbitrage-violating surfaces into smooth, arbitrage-free surfaces. Furthermore, we demonstrate that our model generalizes to data out-of-sample and offers significant improvements in computational performance over traditional methods. We describe the GAN architecture, the synthetic noise generation process, and the training strategy that enables these capabilities. Experimental results demonstrate that our GAN-based approach outperforms traditional smoothing techniques in both accuracy and speed, achieving the lowest mean squared error and runtime. These findings highlight its potential as a fast and reliable solution for denoising implied volatility surfaces at scale.
